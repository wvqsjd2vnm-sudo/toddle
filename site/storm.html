Bosses guard Ironworks, Stormspire and Blackstone Keep. Defeat one to drop a mythic weapon and its vault keycard, then walk the keycard to that vault's round door. The storm hurts 1 to 10 health per second depending on the phase; shields don't block it.

Every match pays out coins and Storm Pass XP: more for eliminations, a top 10 or top 3 finish, a win, bosses and vaults. Spend coins on the Storm Pass (950) or in the item shop, which has new items every day. Coins only come from playing.

On a phone or tablet: left side of the screen moves, right side looks, and the round buttons fire, jump and build. Look at something you built and tap EDIT to change it.
